One small favour — give your pockets a check before loading the washing machine. Coins, keys and lighters go straight into the pump, and a repair is a cost we'd have to pass on. Two seconds saves us both the hassle. Thank you!

Pharmacy & Medical the hospital is around the corner from you

Spitali Amavitaclosest hospital
Rruga Pjetër Budi, opposite the Italian Cultural Institute — a few minutes on foot. Private, English spoken, 24-hour emergency. Go here first for anything that isn't life-threatening. Card payment, and they'll give you an invoice for your insurer.
Spitali Amerikan15 min taxi
The large private hospital, for anything serious or for scans.
Farmaci Arena7 min
Arena Center · 8 AM – 10 PM. There are also several pharmacies within 3–4 minutes on Rruga e Elbasanit and Rruga Pjetër Budi — look for the green cross.
Farmaci Ditë e Natë24 hours
Genuinely round-the-clock chain for the 3 AM emergency. Wolt and Baboon also deliver pharmacy items until late.

Money & Exchange cards work almost everywhere · carry cash for small cafés and taxis

Këmbim Valutor — Rruga e Elbasanit5–8 min
Your nearest exchange windows, plus more around Sheshi Nënë Tereza. Mon–Sat, roughly 8 AM – 7 PM. No commission; the rate is on the board.
The cluster near Skanderbeg Square15 min
A dozen offices within two blocks around Rruga e Barrikadave. Best rates in the city, many open Sundays and until 8 PM.
Open at any hourATMs
There is no true 24-hour exchange desk in central Tirana. After 8 PM use an ATM — and always choose “continue without conversion”, which saves you 5–10%. Bank machines (BKT, Credins, Raiffeisen) beat the standalone Euronet boxes. The airport desk runs for every flight but has the worst rate in the country.

Food & Restaurants

Fishopclosest
Fish and seafood done plainly and done well — pick what looks good and they cook it. Honest prices, no fuss. Our default when we can't be bothered to cook.

Italian bar and restaurant in Blloku. Try the lasagna.

Brunch & Coffee coffee here is a two-hour sit-down, not a takeaway

Inn'sbrunch · by the Arena
Rruga Faik Konica, minutes away. Eggs every way, pancakes, French toast, avocado toast, proper coffee. Go before 11 AM at the weekend or expect a wait.
Mulliri i Vjetërclose · everyday
The local chain Albanians actually use. Opens early, reliable espresso, a counter full of byrek and pastries. Coffee and something warm for under 300 lekë.
Mon Chericlose
Also right nearby, and the nicer place to sit for an hour — proper croissants, cakes, a good cappuccino. For when the morning should take its time.
D'Anversclose
Belgian chocolate house with a café attached. Come for the hot chocolate — thick, dark, unreasonable.

Drinks & Going Out Tirana starts late — bars from eleven, clubs after one

Around the Air Albania Stadium7 min · closest
Your nearest night out, and underrated. The stadium's ground floor and the streets around it are wall-to-wall cafés, lounges and shisha terraces — open very late, mostly locals. On match nights the whole quarter is a party.
Puls11 min · Blloku
The club people name first — big room, loud, packed from one in the morning.
Koni BarRruga Pasho Hysa4.9
Proper cocktail bar and a local favourite — warm room, no attitude, and the best rakija in town. The one to end the night in.
Blloku, generally11 min
Don't plan it. Thirty-odd bars in four blocks, half of them renamed by next season. Walk down Rruga Pjetër Bogdani or Rruga Ismail Qemali after eleven and pick whatever sounds right. Radio Bar and Nouvelle Vague are the reliable ones.
Patala by NagaRruga e Sales
On the city's best bar street (officially Rruga Mustafa Matohiti) — wine bars and small restaurants, one long terrace in summer. 15 minutes, or a 300 lekë taxi.

Culture & Sightseeing

National Archaeological Museum7 min · closest
On Sheshi Nënë Tereza. Small, cheap and a genuinely good hour — Illyrian bronze, Roman mosaics, Butrint finds.
Pyramid of Tirana10 min · free
Climb it for 360° views. Art installations inside, open 24h. Best at sunset.
House of Leaves12 min
Museum of secret surveillance, left much as it was found. The best museum in Tirana. Closed Mondays. 700 lekë.
Bunk'Art 213 min
Cold War bunker museum near Skanderbeg Square. 900 lekë. (Bunk'Art 1, under Dajti, is a 20-minute taxi and half a day.)
