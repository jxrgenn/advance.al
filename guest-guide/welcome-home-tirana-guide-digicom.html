Pharmacy & Medical the hospital is a minute from the door

Spitali Amavita1 minute walk
Rruga Pjetër Budi, opposite the Italian Cultural Institute. A private hospital, and by far the closest place to see a doctor. Start here for anything that isn't a 127 emergency.
Spitali Amerikanby taxi
The large private hospital network, further out. Worth the ride for anything serious.
Farmaci ArenaArena Center
8 AM to 10 PM. There are more pharmacies within a few minutes in every direction. Look for the green cross.
Farmaci Ditë e Natë24 hours
A round-the-clock chain, for the middle of the night. Wolt and Baboon also deliver late.

Money & Exchange cards work almost everywhere · carry cash for small cafés and taxis

Nerguticlosest
Right across from Fishop, so about as close as it gets. The rate is on the board and there is normally no commission.
Around Skanderbeg Squareif you want to compare
Several more exchange offices sit within a couple of blocks of each other near the square, so it is easy to check two rates before changing anything.
After hoursATMs
Exchange offices keep shop hours, so once they close an ATM is the answer. When the machine offers to convert the amount into your own currency, always choose “continue without conversion”: its rate is worse than your bank's. Prefer a bank's own machine over a standalone Euronet box.

Food & Restaurants

Fish and seafood, simply done. The closest proper dinner to the door, and our default when we cannot be bothered to cook.

Beloved Albanian kitchen. Try the lamb shanks and the rice pudding.

Italian bar and restaurant in Blloku. Handmade panini, burgers and Pinsa Romana, from breakfast until late.

Brunch & Coffee coffee here is a sit-down, not a takeaway

Inn'sbrunch
Rruga Faik Konica, just by the Arena. This is the brunch: eggs, pancakes, French toast, big plates and proper coffee. Busy at the weekend, so go early.
Mulliri i Vjetërclose
The local chain Albanians actually use, and one of the closest to you. Opens early, reliable coffee, a counter full of byrek and pastries.
Mon Chericlose
Also right nearby, and the nicer place to sit for an hour over pastries and a coffee.
D'Anversclose
Coffee and chocolate, a short walk away. The one to go to when you want something sweet with it.

Drinks & Going Out Tirana starts late, so nothing gets going before eleven

Around the Air Albania Stadiumclosest
Your nearest night out. The stadium and the streets around it are full of cafés and lounges with big terraces, open late, and it is mostly locals rather than tourists.
PulsBlloku
A club in Blloku, and the one people name first. There are plenty more within a few streets of it.
Koni BarRruga Pasho Hysa4.9
A cocktail bar, and a local favourite. Good place to end the night.
Blloku, generallyBlloku
Do not plan this one. Walk down Rruga Pjetër Bogdani or Rruga Ismail Qemali after eleven and pick whatever sounds right. Radio Bar and Nouvelle Vague are the reliable ones if you want a name.
Patala by NagaRruga e Sales
On Rruga e Sales, a street lined with bars, cafés and restaurants. Worth going for the street as much as for any one place on it.

Culture & Sightseeing

National Archaeological Museumclosest
On Sheshi Nënë Tereza, and the closest museum to you. Small, cheap and worth an hour.
Pyramid of Tiranafree
Climb the steps for views over the whole city. Free, and best at sunset.
House of Leaves
Museum of secret surveillance, in the old Sigurimi building. Closed Mondays. 700 lekë.
Bunk'Art 2
Cold War bunker museum near Skanderbeg Square. 900 lekë. (Bunk'Art 1, out under Dajti, is a taxi ride and most of a day.)
